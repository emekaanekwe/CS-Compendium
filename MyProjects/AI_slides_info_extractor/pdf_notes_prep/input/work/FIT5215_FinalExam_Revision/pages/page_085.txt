Overfitting in Deep NNs
Overfitting
Overfitting occurs when your network models the training data too well and fails to generalize 
to your test (validation) data. 
o Performance measured by errors on “unseen” data.
o Minimize error alone on training data is not enough
o Causes: too many layers, too many hidden nodes, and overtrained. 
Overfit: tendency of the network to “memorize” all 
training samples, leading to poor generalization
