He and Xavier weight initialization

Xavier initialization
o Ensure the variance of the outputs of each 
layer equal to the variance of its inputs
o Also need the gradients to have equal 
variance before and after flowing through a 
layer in the reverse direction
o Good for sigmoid and tanh activation functions
o Not good for ReLU
He initialization
o A variant of Xavier initialization where 𝛼= 1 
o Works better for ReLU.
