He and Xavier weight initialization
Initialisation in deep learning training is crucial:
o Some optimizers can be theoretically guaranteed to converge 
regardless of initializations
o Deep learning algorithms do not have these luxuries:
▪It is iterative, but optimization deep neural networks is not yet well 
understood
▪Initial point is extremely important: it can determine if the algorithm 
converges, or with some bad initialisation, it becomes unstable and fails 
together
What is a good weight/filter initialization?
o Break the ‘symmetry’ of the network: two hidden nodes with the same 
input should have different weights.
o The gradient signal to flow well in both directions and don’t want the 
signal to die out or to explode and saturate.
o Large initial weights has better symmetry breaking effect, help 
avoiding losing signals and redundant units, but could result in 
exploding values during backward and forward passes, especially in 
Recurrent Neural Networks.
Initialization is important for training DL models.
